## How fast the queue moves

A queue drains at roughly **0.9 entries per overall pick made** — nearly 1:1,
because the names near the top of your queue are the same names everyone else
is drafting. So your position in the list advances about 11-13 entries between
each of your turns, not 1. Measured over 1000 simulated drafts:

| Your pick | Overall # | Queue entry you land on (median) |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | 7 | 2 |
| R2 | 18 | 11 |
| R3 | 31 | 25 |
| R4 | 42 | 33 |
| R5 | 55 | 45 |
| R6 | 66 | 52 |
| R7 | 79 | 68 |
| R8 | 90 | 78 |
| R9 | 103 | 92 |
| R10 | 114 | 96 |

This is why QB sits around entry 50-60 and TE around 61-73: those are the
entries actually reached in rounds 6 and 7. Pushing them deeper does not buy
a later pick, it just means every startable QB and TE is gone — tested, and
the median QB fell from board rank #60 to #265.
